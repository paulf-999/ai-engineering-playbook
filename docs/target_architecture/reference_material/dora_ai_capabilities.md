# 📊 DORA: AI Capabilities Model

For anyone shaping the target architecture: what DORA's (DevOps Research and Assessment) AI Capabilities Model found and measured, where its limits are, and how far each capability applies to a personal playbook.

- **Source:** [*DORA AI Capabilities Model*](https://dora.dev/ai/capabilities-model/report/), v. 2025.1, Google LLC, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with citations retrieved October 2025 (p. 1).
- **Read:** in full on 2026-11-02, from the PDF the report page links to.
- **Page numbers:** "p." means the report's printed page number, which is one less than the PDF page.
- **Methodology:** this report defers to the [*State of AI-assisted Software Development 2025*](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/) report for its methods and models (p. 94).
- **Labels:** the overview, capabilities, findings and limitations pages report the source, while the applicability page is this playbook's interpretation.

## 📋 Executive Summary

- **What it is:** DORA's 2025 research, drawing on nearly 5,000 technology professionals, that names seven capabilities shaping how much teams gain from AI (p. 3).
- **Core idea:** "AI's primary role in software development is to amplify", magnifying an organisation's strengths and dysfunctions alike (p. 3).
- **Caution:** the evidence comes from surveys and shows correlation, so treat each finding as a hypothesis to test.
- **For this playbook:** curated internal context for AI, strong version control and small batches apply directly, while the other four capabilities are mainly organisational.
