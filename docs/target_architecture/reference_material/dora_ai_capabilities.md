# 📊 DORA: AI Capabilities Model

For anyone shaping the target architecture: what DORA's (DevOps Research and Assessment) AI Capabilities Model found and measured, where its limits are, and how far each capability applies to a personal playbook.

## 📋 Executive Summary

- **What it is:** DORA's 2025 research, drawing on nearly 5,000 technology professionals, naming seven capabilities that shape how much teams gain from AI (p. 3).
- **Core idea:** "AI's primary role in software development is to amplify", magnifying an organisation's strengths and dysfunctions alike (p. 3).
- **Caution:** the evidence comes from surveys and shows correlation, so treat each finding as a hypothesis to test.
- **Direct fit:** three capabilities apply directly to a personal playbook: giving AI curated internal context, strong version control, and working in small, frequent changes.
- **Organisational:** the other four depend mainly on an organisation, so they apply here only indirectly or not at all.
