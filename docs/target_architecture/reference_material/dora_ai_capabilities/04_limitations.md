# ⚠️ DORA report: Limitations

**Purpose:** what DORA's evidence can and can't show.

This page is part of the [summary of DORA's AI Capabilities Model](../dora_ai_capabilities.md), and "p." means the report's printed page, one less than the PDF page.

## 📏 Strength of the evidence

These limits come from how the data was gathered:

- 🔗 **Correlation, not cause:** the report's single survey can show that a capability goes with better results, but not that it causes them.
  - **Estimates:** effects are shown with 89% credible intervals, the range the true effect most likely falls in (p. 10).
- 📢 **Strong wording:** the report calls the capabilities "proven to amplify" (p. 4), which goes further than such a survey can show.
- 🙋 **Self-report:** outcomes such as individual effectiveness and code quality are respondents' own judgements.

## 🧭 Scope and source

These limits affect how far the findings transfer here:

- 🪜 **Not stages:** the seven capabilities sit side by side, rather than forming a ladder to climb in order.
- 👥 **Teams, not individuals:** respondents describe their teams and organisations, so findings don't transfer directly to one person's setup.
- 🏢 **Publisher:** Google Cloud runs DORA, and some examples use its own tools, such as `GEMINI.md`.
