# 🧩 DORA report: The seven capabilities

For anyone shaping the target architecture: the seven capabilities, what each amplifies, and where they appear in the report.

- **Part of:** [DORA: AI Capabilities Model](../dora_ai_capabilities.md).
- **DORA:** DevOps Research and Assessment, a Google Cloud research programme on software delivery performance.
- **Page numbers:** "p." is the report's printed page number, one less than the PDF page.

| Capability | DORA's definition, paraphrased | Amplifies AI's effect on | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear and communicated AI stance | • **What:** an official position on AI use that is comprehensible and widely known.<br>• **Covers:** expectation, support, permission and applicability. | • **Amplifies:** individual effectiveness, organisational performance and throughput.<br>• **Reduces:** friction. | 14–19 |
| Healthy data ecosystems | • **What:** internal data that is high-quality, easily accessible and unified. | • **Amplifies:** organisational performance. | 20–25 |
| AI-accessible internal data | • **What:** AI securely connected to internal code, docs and current external docs.<br>• **Term:** the report calls this context engineering. | • **Amplifies:** individual effectiveness and code quality. | 26–35 |
| Strong version control practices | • **What:** everything in version control, with frequent small commits.<br>• **Rollback:** confident use of undo and revert. | • **Commit frequency:** amplifies individual effectiveness.<br>• **Rollback:** amplifies team performance. | 36–43 |
| Working in small batches | • **What:** work split into the smallest chunks that can be completed and delivered independently. | • **Amplifies:** product performance.<br>• **Reduces:** friction. | 44–51 |
| User-centric focus | • **What:** team priorities aligned to what users need. | • **Amplifies:** team performance.<br>• **Warning:** team performance falls when user focus is low. | 52–57 |
| Quality internal platforms | • **What:** shared tools, services, docs and golden paths, run as an internal product.<br>• **Golden paths:** ready-made, supported ways to do common tasks. | • **Amplifies:** organisational performance.<br>• **Condition:** the effect is negligible when platform quality is low. | 58–65 |
