# 🏛️ The TOGAF Standard

**Purpose:** what the TOGAF Standard is, the method and vocabulary it gives enterprise architecture, and how its parts fit together.

## 📋 Executive Summary

- 🏛️ **What it is:** an open framework from The Open Group for designing, planning and governing an organisation's architecture.
- 🔁 **Core idea:** a repeatable method, the Architecture Development Method (ADM), takes an organisation from its current architecture to a target one.
- 🧩 **Shared language:** it defines terms such as building block, viewpoint and view, so architects and stakeholders mean the same thing.
- 📦 **Content:** it says what an architecture effort produces and where the results are kept for reuse.
- ✂️ **Tailoring:** it expects every organisation to adapt the method rather than follow it as written.

## 🔗 Source

The standard and how to cite it:

- 📄 **Standard:** [*The TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition*](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf), The Open Group, 2022.
- 🧱 **Structure:** the 10th Edition splits into Fundamental Content, which these pages cover, and optional Series Guides on specific topics.
