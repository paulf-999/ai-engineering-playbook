# 🗣️ Personal voice

**Purpose:** how Claude drafts short text the maintainer will send as their own, such as a PR's "Why", a comment or an email.

Drawn from 5 of the posts on [the maintainer's Medium page](https://medium.com/@paul-fry). Blog posts follow [`blog_posts.md`](blog_posts.md) instead.

## ✍️ How it sounds

- 👤 **Person:** "I" for experience and opinion, "you" for instructions, and rarely "we".
- 💬 **Contractions:** use them throughout, such as "it's", "don't" and "I'd".
- 📏 **Sentences:** about 15 to 20 words in prose and 6 to 12 in bullets, in paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences.
- 🎯 **Opening:** say plainly what it's about, as in "This post explains how to…", or state the problem, as in "Manually managing Snowflake resources, grants and privileges at scale is difficult, and stressful!"
- 🧱 **Bullets:** a bold label, a colon and a short explanation, for reasons or options.
- 💡 **Opinions:** "I'd recommend…", and "It's less about X, more about Y" at most once.
- ⚠️ **Limits:** follow a claim with its caveat, as in "Claude can still skip it, so keep critical rules always-on."
- 🔎 **Asides:** put examples and admissions in brackets, as in "(e.g., dev, prod)" or "(and lots of trial and error)".
- 😏 **Humour:** at most one dry, self-directed aside, and none in figures or caveats.

## 🚫 What to avoid

- 🪧 **Slogans:** no punchy one-liners, stacked short sentences or "not X, but Y" as a flourish.
- 😀 **Emoji:** none in the text itself.
- ❗ **Exclamation marks:** only for a sign-off or a rare emphasis.
- 🧾 **Invented experience:** never claim the maintainer ran, read, felt or decided something they haven't said, and leave a blank for it instead.

## ✅ Before it's sent

- 🖊️ **Their edit:** show the draft for the maintainer to reword, since matching the voice doesn't make it their own words.
- 🏷️ **Label:** if it's used unchanged where own words are asked for, say Claude drafted it.
