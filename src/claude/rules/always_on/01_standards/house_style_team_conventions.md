# 🇬🇧 House style

**Purpose:** this team's spelling and naming choices.

- 🇬🇧 **British spelling:** use -ise and other British forms in prose, but keep existing identifiers and filenames as they are.
- 🔤 **snake_case:** name files and folders in lowercase words joined by underscores, with no hyphens or spaces, and keep existing names as they are.
