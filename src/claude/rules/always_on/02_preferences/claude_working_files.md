# 🗂️ Working files

**Purpose:** where Claude keeps your TODOs, drafts and notes.

- 💾 **Checkpoints:** once a task spans many files or turns, write its decisions and open items to `_sessions/YYYY_MM_DD_<topic>.md` in your Claude config directory (`$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, or `~/.claude` when unset), as an absolute path.
