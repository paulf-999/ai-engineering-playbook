# 🇬🇧 House style

**Purpose:** this team's own writing and naming choices. Another team can replace them with its own, while the standards stay in `writing_style.md` and `naming_standards.md`.

- 🇬🇧 **British spelling:** use -ise and other British forms in prose, but keep existing identifiers and filenames as they are. A user who wants American English says so.
- 🔤 **snake_case:** name files and folders in lowercase words joined by underscores, with no hyphens or spaces, and keep existing names as they are.
