# 🔄 SDLC stages

**Purpose:** one index of how to work in each stage of the software development lifecycle (SDLC), with the page to read for each.

The pages distil Anthropic's [AI-native SDLC article](https://claude.com/resources/articles/the-ai-native-sdlc-playbook) and [course](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-native-sdlc-playbook). Where they differ from another rule, that rule wins. Governance and Measures sections guide people, and nothing enforces or collects them.

## 📚 Read on demand

- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_plan_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_plan_stage.md), in your Claude config directory, when turning an idea, ticket or alert into an intent.
- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_design_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_design_stage.md), when turning an intent into a spec or mock-up.
- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_build_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_build_stage.md), when writing an implementation plan, building from one or running parallel sessions.
- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_test_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_test_stage.md), before planning or making a bug fix, or when setting up checks or evals.
- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_deploy_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_deploy_stage.md), when reviewing a change, setting an approval gate, or releasing or rolling back.
- [`on_demand/01_standards/ai_sdlc_stages/_maintain_stage.md`](ai_sdlc_stages/_maintain_stage.md), when handling an alert, incident or scheduled scan.
