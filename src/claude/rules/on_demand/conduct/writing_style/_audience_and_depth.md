# 👥 Writing style: Audience and depth

**Purpose:** how to match technical depth to the reader, and open a page so a newcomer can follow it.

## ✍️ Before writing

- 🎯 **Confirm the audience:** before writing for people outside the repo, confirm who it's for and set the depth from the table below.
- ❓ **Ask when unsure:** if the audience is unknown or spans several groups, ask first.

| Audience | Focus | Technical level | Emojis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💼 **Management** | Progress, decisions and risks | None, and no jargon | None |
| 🔭 **Technical management** | Strategy and outcomes | Concepts, not mechanics | Headings only |
| 📊 **Data analytics team** | What the data means | Partial | Headings only |
| 🗄️ **Data engineering team** | Implementation, such as Airflow, dbt and Snowflake | Full | Headings only |
| ⚙️ **Infrastructure and DevOps** | Infrastructure and operations | Full | Headings only |

## 🚪 Openings

- 🎯 **Purpose first:** put `**Purpose:**` and one sentence on what the page covers on the line after the H1.
- 📋 **Executive Summary:** open long-form content, such as a report or Confluence page, with 3 to 5 bullets that give the whole idea.
- 🗣️ **Newcomer test:** reread each sentence as someone who has never seen the repo, and rewrite any they couldn't follow.
