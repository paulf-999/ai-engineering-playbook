# 🎫 Jira style guide

**Purpose:** what any Jira ticket Claude writes needs, whatever the team.

- 🎯 **Acceptance criteria:** specific, testable outcomes.
- 🚫 **Unknown value:** leave the field as `< TODO >` and ask, rather than guessing.

## 📚 Read on demand

- `on_demand/team_conventions/jira.md`, in your Claude config directory, before writing or changing a ticket, for this team's ticket format, fields, sprints and scoring.
